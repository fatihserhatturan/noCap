What it answers

Questions you used to answer by ear.

Flow is the hardest part of rap to put into words. noCap turns it into something you can point at.

Density

How dense is the flow?

Every bar is a row, every beat a column. Syllables land exactly where they were rapped, so double-time runs, triplets and breathing room are obvious at a glance.

  • Syllables per beat, per bar and per section
  • Peak and average density, bar-by-bar ranking
  • Syncopation score — how often you land off the grid
Pocket

Ahead, on, or behind the beat?

noCap measures each syllable's offset from the grid, then summarizes it as pocket and timing quality — the difference between laid-back and rushing, in numbers.

  • Pocket score with average and range of offsets
  • Timing variance and delivery consistency
  • Compare bars by density, sync, stress, pocket or timing
Rhymes

Where do the rhymes land?

Phonetic matching groups perfect, slant, assonance and internal rhymes, then color-codes them on the grid. Follow a rhyme chain across bars and see how the scheme is built.

  • Multi-syllable and internal rhyme detection
  • Click a group to highlight it across the whole track
  • Average rhyme-chain length as a metric
Report

Break it down, section by section.

The Report page puts every metric in one place, charts density and pocket across the whole track and splits it into sections. Click any bar on the chart to open it in the flow view, or any syllable on the grid to hear it.

  • Click-to-play with original mix or vocals only
  • Section table with density, sync, tempo and energy
  • Export the flow map as PNG or SVG
Lyrics

Read it, fix it, take it with you.

The Lyrics page shows the whole track as a lyric sheet, one bar per line, with every rhyme in its colour. If a word was misheard, fix it there and the flow map and metrics update with it.

  • Sections and bar numbers; click a line to open it in the flow view
  • Edit line by line, restore the original any time
  • Export as text, synced .lrc or word-timed .lrc

How it works

From audio file to flow map in four steps.

  1. 01

    Drop a track

    MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, M4A or OGG. Beat grid and BPM are detected from the waveform.

  2. 02

    Isolate the vocals

    Demucs separates the vocal stem so the beat doesn't confuse transcription.

  3. 03

    Transcribe every word

    Whisper produces word-level timestamps; filters drop invented words and a second pass listens again to anything it skipped.

  4. 04

    Snap to the grid

    Words are split into syllables with stress from the CMU dictionary and placed on the beat grid.

While it works, noCap shows each step in plain words, how long is left and what it has found so far, and you can cancel at any point.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. noCap is MIT-licensed open source. There's no account, subscription or usage limit.

Does my audio get uploaded anywhere?

No. Beat detection, vocal separation and transcription all run on your computer. Tracks and analyses are stored locally in your library.

Which computers are supported?

There are self-contained builds for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) and for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. On Windows, an NVIDIA graphics card speeds up analysis; without one it runs on the processor, just slower. On Intel Macs or Linux you can run noCap from source today — see the developer setup. Prebuilt packages for more platforms are welcome as contributions.

How do I install it on Windows?

The Windows download comes in two parts because of GitHub's file size limit. Download both .7z.001 and .7z.002 from the latest release into the same folder, open the .001 file with 7-Zip and extract it, then run noCap.exe. The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time: choose More info → Run anyway.

Why is the download so large?

The app bundles everything it needs — Python, PyTorch, Demucs and the Whisper model — so it works offline with no setup. The trade-off is a big one-time download.

macOS says the app can't be opened. What do I do?

Right-click (or Control-click) noCap in Applications and choose Open, then confirm. You only need to do this once.

Does it work with lyrics in languages other than English?

noCap uses Whisper's English model and the CMU (English) pronouncing dictionary, so English-language tracks give the best results today. Multilingual support is on the wish list.

How long does an analysis take?

A few minutes for a typical song on an Apple Silicon Mac; separating the vocals and listening to the lyrics are the heavy steps. Progress and time left are shown live, and you can cancel at any point. Once analyzed, a track reopens instantly from your library.

What if a word is transcribed wrong?

Open the Lyrics page and fix it. noCap re-places the corrected words on the beat grid, so the flow map, rhymes and metrics update too, and you can restore the original at any time.
